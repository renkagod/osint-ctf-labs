# Lost Altar — Phase briefs

## Phase 1

At the end of WWII, part of an altar disappeared. The building where it stood vanished too — the site was paved over, and the city was later renamed. Find where the altar **used to stand** (its former home).

**Submit:** Wikipedia name in the **local language**; replace spaces with `~`.

## Phase 2

The altar's "ringing voice" was saved from destruction and moved to a place that shelters thousands of similar silent voices. Find where this voice was taken.

**Submit:** Wikipedia name; replace spaces with `@`.

## Phase 3

The voice is signed in Latin. Find the year it was given a **second life** (restoration / reopening).

**Submit:** year in Roman numerals.
